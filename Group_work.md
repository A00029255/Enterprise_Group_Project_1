# Historical development

The history of gaming represents one of the most remarkable technological and cultural evolutions in computing. What began as simple programming experiments has grown into a global industry that work with: art, entertainment, science, and business. The development of games can be traced through several major eras, each defined by innovations in hardware, software, and the gaming experience.

### Early Origins: 1950s–1970s

The roots of digital games lie in early computer research and experiments. In the 1950s, scientists and engineers used minimal computing power to create simple interactive programs as demonstrations of technology. One of the earliest examples is  OXO (1952) by Alexander S. who simulated a game of tic-tac-toe on the EDSAC computer. In 1958, physicist William Higinbotham created Tennis for Two, displayed on an oscilloscope, which is often referred to as the first true interactive video game.
